# Refaat Al Ktifan, Founder and CEO

Refaat Al Ktifan is the Founder and CEO of Oronts GmbH. He has fifteen plus years building enterprise systems across Europe and the Middle East, spanning full-stack development, cloud architecture, and AI integration. He founded Oronts in Munich in 2023.

## Languages

Refaat works in Arabic as a native speaker, in German at a near-native level, and in English at a professional level. That range supports Oronts delivery in German, English, and Arabic across the EU and the Gulf.

## Technical Philosophy

His stated view is that good architecture is defined by what it makes easy to change. He holds a strong position on code ownership: if a client cannot run and modify and deploy it without us, we have failed. That principle runs through the Oronts promise of code you own and no vendor lock-in, and through the 90-Day Production Pilot, where repositories land in the client organization from day 1.

## Honesty Stance

Refaat is deliberate about what Oronts claims in public. Some enterprise delivery happens through partner and agency structures where client names cannot be disclosed, so he does not publish logos or case claims he cannot verify. The public site names only what can be verified: Oronts products such as OGuardAI, PimTx, Vendure Data Hub, and Pimcore Asset Pilot, and the one named client build, Exfinity GmbH, where he is the Lead AI Solution Architect.

## Background

His background is senior enterprise engineering, including delivery through partner and agency structures, with a deep Pimcore, Symfony, commerce, cloud, and AI practice. That practice shows up in the Oronts pillars, in the Pimcore and Vendure platform work, and in production AI systems built on Mastra, RAG, and agentic AI, engineered to meet GDPR and the EU AI Act.
