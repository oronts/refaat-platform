# The Oronts Brand

The brand of Oronts GmbH, the Munich software engineering company led by Refaat Al Ktifan, is built to feel calm, premium, and confident. It earns credibility by describing the work rather than reaching for adjectives.

## Visual identity

The core palette is a dark navy, #1d233a, paired with purple in two tones, #7432ff and #8b5cf6. The typeface is Inter. Together they give the site and its materials a restrained, technical, premium look that matches the tone of the writing. The palette carries across the website locales, which include English, German, Spanish, French, and Arabic, with Arabic rendered right to left.

## Voice

The voice is senior and factual. It avoids hype and lets the substance of the engineering carry the credibility. This is the same register used across the eight core services, the platform practices for Pimcore, Vendure, Payload CMS, and n8n, the RAG and Production AI pages, and the 67 technical guides authored by the Oronts Engineering Team.

## The recurring promise

The brand returns to one promise: code you own and no vendor lock-in. It appears in the positioning line, we build, you own and control, and it runs through the values of no black-box dependencies, no forced upgrades or surprise pricing, and no code you cannot take with you. The same principle shapes the products, from OGuardAI and PimTx to Vendure Data Hub and the Oronts AI Assistant, and it defines the 90-Day Production Pilot, where repositories land in the client organization on day one. The brand voice is consistent with how Oronts builds: agentic AI on Mastra, RAG systems with citations, and enterprise platforms delivered under GDPR and the EU AI Act.
