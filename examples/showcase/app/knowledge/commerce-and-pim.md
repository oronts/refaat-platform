# Commerce, PIM, and Data Platforms at Oronts

Commerce, PIM, and Data Platforms is one of the three practice pillars at Oronts GmbH, the Munich software studio founded in 2023 by Refaat Al Ktifan. It covers headless commerce and product data engineering with deep Pimcore and Vendure practices, built on Oronts own published plugins. The recurring promise holds here too: code you own and no vendor lock-in.

## E-Commerce and PIM Service

The E-Commerce and PIM service builds complete e-commerce ecosystems with Product Information Management for both B2B and B2C, spanning custom platforms, PIM, multi-channel distribution, payments, and order management.

## The Pimcore Practice

The Pimcore practice covers PIM and MDM implementation, migration to Pimcore 2026.1 and Studio, POCL license assessment, custom bundle development, and integration with ERP, shop, and marketplace systems, plus performance and scaling. Oronts offers a fixed-price Pimcore 2026 readiness audit. The Pimcore Asset Pilot Bundle, published at github.com/oronts/pimcore-asset-pilot-bundle, adds rule-based digital asset organization with configurable rules, priority ordering, and audit logging. PimTx makes edits for Pimcore 11 and 12 safe and traceable when many people and automated jobs change the same data at once, with cooperative locks, version guards, field ownership, idempotency, and a full audit trail.

## The Vendure Practice

The Vendure practice delivers full commerce builds, custom TypeScript plugins, B2B on Vendure Core with contract pricing, quotes, and approvals, and migration and replatforming with ERP and PIM integration, alongside a fixed-scope Vendure architecture review. Two products back it. Vendure Data Hub, at github.com/oronts/vendure-data-hub-plugin, is an enterprise ETL and data integration plugin with a visual pipeline builder, extractors, transform operators, entity loaders, and feed generators for Google Merchant and Amazon. Vendure Customer Intelligence is a proprietary plugin uniting wishlists, reviews, loyalty points, and cart recovery as one domain.
