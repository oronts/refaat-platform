# Production AI and the RAG Practice

Production AI and Automation is one of the three practice pillars at Oronts GmbH, the Munich software engineering company led by Refaat Al Ktifan. The pillar covers agent systems, RAG, and AI workflows that survive production: governed, observable, and honest about what is real.

## The RAG taxonomy

Oronts treats RAG as a full taxonomy that builds toward a Structured Agentic RAG target. The catalog spans Naive or Standard RAG, GraphRAG, Hybrid, Hierarchical or RAPTOR, Multimodal, Agentic, Corrective or CRAG, Self-RAG, Adaptive, Conversational, Contextual Retrieval, and HyDE. Every variant is delivered with grounding, citations, permission-aware retrieval, guardrails, and audit, so answers stay traceable to their sources.

## Production AI pillars

The Production AI page organizes the discipline into agentic AI and tool calling, evaluation and testing with Ragas or TruLens and LLM as judge and CI gates, model optimization with LoRA and QLoRA and quantization and open models, LLMOps with gateways and semantic caching and structured output and PII and prompt-injection guardrails, and advanced context management. The Enterprise AI foundation stays model neutral, built on gateways, knowledge graphs, agentic operations, and compliance guardrails aligned to GDPR and the EU AI Act.

## Agentic Frameworks

The Agentic Frameworks practice covers agent design, tool and API integration, retrieval, human in the loop and guardrails, and evaluation and observability. It is provider agnostic over OpenAI and Anthropic and runs on Mastra, LangGraph, CrewAI, and the Vercel AI SDK. The proof is the Oronts AI Assistant, the live agent system running in production on the website, built on Mastra with five agents and four tools, streaming responses, and a hardened API layer that never invents prices.
