# The Oronts Technology Ecosystem

Oronts GmbH, the Munich software engineering company founded in 2023 by Refaat Al Ktifan, builds with a broad ecosystem of clouds, models, data systems, and frameworks. The names below are the technologies Oronts engineers work with, not clients or references. They form the toolbox behind the eight core services and the 90-Day Production Pilot, and behind products like OGuardAI, PimTx, and the Vendure Data Hub.

## Clouds and Infrastructure

Delivery runs across AWS, Azure, and GCP. Containers and orchestration use Docker and Kubernetes, with GitHub Actions driving CI and CD. Observability and edge rely on Datadog, Sentry, and Cloudflare.

## Models and AI

Model work draws on OpenAI, Anthropic, and Hugging Face. These providers sit behind the RAG and agentic AI practice and the Oronts AI Assistant, the live Mastra agent system on the website. The practice stays provider agnostic across OpenAI and Anthropic so clients are never locked to a single model vendor.

## Data Systems

Data platforms are built on PostgreSQL, Snowflake, and Kafka, supporting the Data Engineering service across pipelines, warehousing, and real-time analytics.

## Commerce and PIM

Commerce and product data work centers on Pimcore, Vendure, and Akeneo, the foundation for the Commerce, PIM, and Data Platforms pillar.

## Languages and Frameworks

Across the practice Oronts writes TypeScript, Node.js, and NestJS, PHP and Symfony, and Go, with React and Next.js on the frontend and GraphQL and REST for APIs. This spread lets Oronts meet enterprise systems where they are while keeping every build to GDPR and the EU AI Act, and while honoring the standing commitment that clients can run, modify, and deploy their code without Oronts.
