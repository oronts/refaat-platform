# Oronts GmbH: Company Overview

Oronts GmbH is a Munich-based enterprise software engineering company, a founder-led software studio founded in 2023 by Refaat Al Ktifan. Its positioning is direct: we build, you own and control. Oronts delivers senior AI, software, and commerce engineering from Munich, with a recurring promise of code you own and no vendor lock-in.

## What Oronts Builds

The work spans agentic AI, full-stack development, headless e-commerce, and cloud infrastructure, taking a use case from pilot to production in 90 days through the 90-Day Production Pilot. The taglines used across the site are Transforming Business with AI and Your Gateway to Modern IT and Software Solutions.

## The Three Practice Pillars

Oronts organizes production-ready systems clients fully own into three pillars. Production AI and Automation covers agent systems, RAG, and AI workflows that survive production, governed, observable, and honest about what is real. Enterprise Software and Integration covers custom platforms, APIs, and integrations engineered for ownership, with documented architecture, tests, and handover. Commerce, PIM, and Data Platforms covers headless commerce and product data engineering with deep Pimcore and Vendure practices, built on the company's own published plugins.

## Why Oronts Exists

The mission is to help companies escape fragile systems and AI hype and replace them with software they actually own and control. The values follow from that: no black-box dependencies, no forced upgrades or surprise pricing, no code you cannot take with you. Oronts states its standards plainly, as standards rather than audited metrics: 100 percent client code ownership and IP from the first commit, a typical 90 day pilot window, a one business day senior response, and zero vendor lock-in. The brand voice is calm, premium, and confident, drawing credibility from describing the work rather than from adjectives.
