# The Oronts Technical Guides Library

Oronts GmbH publishes 67 technical guides across five locales. They are authored by the Oronts Engineering Team and written for CTOs, engineering leads, and senior developers. The library reflects the Munich studio's practice in agentic AI, RAG, commerce, and cloud engineering, and it carries the same brand voice: credibility from describing the work rather than adjectives.

## AI systems and architecture

This group covers how to design and operate production agent systems. Representative titles include the Enterprise Guide to Agentic AI Systems, Multi-Agent Architecture, AI Orchestration, AI Failure Modes, AI Observability, Designing AI Without Vendor Lock-In, and a Mastra production teardown of how the website assistant works.

## RAG and retrieval

These guides go deep on retrieval quality. Titles include Enterprise RAG Systems, RAG Is Not Enough, Evaluating RAG Quality, and Vector Search Architecture.

## AI governance, security, PII, and compliance

This theme addresses trust and regulation. Titles include AI Governance, AI Decision Traceability, Prompt Injection Defense for Agentic Systems, MCP in Production, GDPR-Compliant AI, PII-safe RAG with Tokenization, EU AI Act for the Mittelstand, and EU AI Act after the 2026 Digital Omnibus.

## Commerce, PIM, and product data

Here the focus is on Pimcore and Vendure practice. Titles include E-commerce Platforms, Commerce Search Architecture, Headless Commerce Beyond the Frontend, From SAP to Shop, Pimcore Upgrade 10 to 12, Vendure Production Architecture, and Vendure Core versus Platform.

## Software, systems, and cloud engineering

This group covers architecture and operations. Titles include System Architecture and Scalability, API Design for Long-Term Systems, Domain-Driven Design in Reality, Concurrency and Data Integrity, Event-Driven Architecture in Practice, Multi-Tenant System Design, AWS Serverless Multi-Tenant SaaS, Kubernetes for SaaS Platforms, Observability for Modern Systems, and OpenTelemetry Production Patterns.
